# Zakat Mal: Comprehensive Fiqh & Mathematical Calculation Protocol

This skill provides an authoritative, peer-reviewed, and mathematically rigorous specification for calculating **Zakat Mal** (Zakat on Wealth) across all recognized Shariah asset classes. Every formula, nisab threshold, hawl condition, and deduction is grounded in authentic texts of the **Al-Qur'an**, **Sahih Hadith**, **Ijma'**, **AAOIFI Shari'ah Standard No. 35**, **Fatwa Majelis Ulama Indonesia (MUI)**, and **Peraturan BAZNAS**.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Zakat Mal

In Islamic jurisprudence, Zakat Mal is obligatory upon wealth meeting five universal conditions:
1. **Al-Milkiyyah at-Tammah (Absolute/Complete Ownership)**: The owner has complete rights and free disposal of the asset.
2. **An-Nama' (Productive / Growth-Capable Potential)**: The asset actually grows or has the inherent capacity to generate yield or value.
3. **Bulugh an-Nisab (Reaching the Nisab Threshold)**: The minimum quantity/value of wealth that triggers zakat liability.
4. **Fadhl 'an al-Hajah al-Ashliyyah (Excess over Essential Needs)**: Beyond basic subsistence for the owner and their dependents (food, clothing, shelter, health, essential transport).
5. **Hawlan al-Hawl (Completion of One Full Year)**: Passing of 1 year in possession (applicable to monetary wealth, gold/silver, business, and livestock; **exempt** from agriculture, rikaz, and income per contemporary qiyas upon harvest).
6. **Salamah min ad-Dayn (Free from Immediate Debt)**: Immediate short-term debts due are deducted from liquid zakatable assets.

---

## 2. Hijri (Lunar) vs. Gregorian (Solar) Calendar Rates

* **Standard Islamic Lunar Year (Hijri)**: ~354.36 days. Standard Zakat rate is **2.500%** ($\frac{1}{40}$).
* **Gregorian Solar Year (Solar/Fiscal Calendar)**: ~365.25 days.
* **AAOIFI Shari'ah Standard No. 35 (Section 4/1/4)**:
  $$\text{Adjusted Solar Rate} = 2.5\% \times \frac{365.25}{354.36} \approx \mathbf{2.577\%} \quad (0.02577)$$

## 3. Asset Categories, Nisab, Rates & Dalil Matrix

### A. Gold, Silver, Cash & Monetary Assets (An-Naqdayn)

1. **Emas (Gold)**:
   - **Nisab**: 20 Mithqal / Dinar = **85 grams** of pure gold (24 karat).
   - **Rate**: 2.5% (Hijri) or 2.577% (Gregorian).
   - **Condition**: 1 Hawl.
   - **Personal Jewelry ('Urf)**: Reasonable personal adornment without extravagance is exempt according to Jumhur (Maliki, Syafi'i, Hanbali). Investment, storage, or jewelry exceeding customary extravagance is subject to Zakat.

2. **Perak (Silver)**:
   - **Nisab**: 200 Dirhams = 5 Uqiyah = **595 grams** of pure silver.
   - **Rate**: 2.5% (Hijri) or 2.577% (Gregorian).
   - **Condition**: 1 Hawl.

3. **Uang Simpanan, Tabungan, Deposito, Giro (Cash & Bank Deposits)**:
   - Evaluated against the **Gold Nisab (85g)** (or Silver Nisab 595g as the more conservative option for the poor per Hanafi school).
   - Formula:
     $$\text{Zakatable Balance} = (\text{Total Liquid Cash \& Bank Balances}) - (\text{Immediate Due Debts})$$
     $$\text{If } \text{Zakatable Balance} \ge \text{Nisab} \implies \text{Zakat} = \text{Zakatable Balance} \times 2.5\%$$

**Authoritative Dalil:**
- **Al-Qur'an At-Tawbah (9:34-35)**:
  > وَالَّذِينَ يَكْنِزُونَ الذَّهَبَ وَالْفِضَّةَ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَهَا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ
  > *"And those who hoard gold and silver and spend it not in the way of Allah - give them tidings of a painful punishment."*
- **Sahih Bukhari (No. 1405), Sahih Muslim (No. 979)**:
  > لَيْسَ فِيمَا دُونَ خَمْسِ أَوَاقٍ صَدَقَةٌ
  > *"There is no charity (zakat) on anything less than five Uqiyah [200 dirhams / 595g silver]."*
- **Sunan Abu Dawud (No. 1573)**, Hasan/Sahih:
  > فإذا كانت لك مائتا درهم وحال عليها الحول ففيها خمسة دراهم وليس عليك شيء يعني في الذهب حتى يكون لك عشرون دينارا فإذا كانت لك عشرون دينارا وحال عليها الحول ففيها نصف دينار
  > *"When you possess two hundred dirhams and a year passes, five dirhams are due. And you owe nothing on gold until you possess twenty dinars. When you possess twenty dinars and a year passes, half a dinar is due."*

### B. Zakat Perdagangan / Perniagaan ('Urud at-Tijarah / Commercial Goods)

- **Scope**: Any assets acquired with the intention of sale and profit (inventory, wholesale/retail goods, real estate held for flipping).
- **Nisab**: Equivalent to 85g pure gold.
- **Hawl**: 1 year.
- **Valuation**: Current fair market selling value at the end of the Hawl (not historical cost).
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Zakat Base} = (\text{Cash \& Bank Balances}) + (\text{Inventory at Market Value}) + (\text{Good Receivables/Piutang Lancar}) - (\text{Current Due Operational Debts})$$
  $$\text{If } \text{Zakat Base} \ge \text{Nisab} \implies \text{Zakat} = \text{Zakat Base} \times 2.5\% \text{ (or } 2.577\%\text{)}$$

**Authoritative Dalil:**
- **Al-Qur'an Al-Baqarah (2:267)**:
  > يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَنفِقُوا مِن طَيِّبَاتِ مَا كَسَبْتُمْ
  > *"O you who have believed, spend from the good things which you have earned..."*
- **Sunan Abu Dawud (No. 1562)**:
  > كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَأْمُرُنَا أَنْ نُخْرِجَ الصَّدَقَةَ مِنَ الَّذِي نُعِدُّ لِلْبَيْعِ
  > *"The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) commanded us to pay zakat from whatever we prepared for sale."*

### C. Zakat Pendapatan / Profesi (Professional Income / Earnings)

- **Legitimacy**: Established by the **International Islamic Fiqh Academy**, **Yusuf Al-Qaradawi (*Fiqh az-Zakat*)**, and **Fatwa MUI No. 3 Tahun 2003**.
- **Timing**:
  - Paid upon receipt of income (analogous to agricultural harvest: *Qiyas 'ala az-Zuru'*, Al-An'am 141), or accumulated over 1 year (*Qiyas 'ala an-Naqdayn*).
- **Nisab**:
  - Annual: 85g gold price.
  - Monthly: $\frac{85\text{g Gold Price}}{12} \approx 7.083\text{g gold equivalent per month}$.
- **Approaches**:
  1. **Gross Approach (Bruto)**:
     $$\text{Zakat} = \text{Gross Income} \times 2.5\% \quad (\text{if Gross} \ge \text{Nisab})$$
  2. **Net Approach (Netto - Recommended by MUI & Fiqh Academy)**:
     $$\text{Net Income} = \text{Gross Income} - \text{Essential Living Costs (Hajah Ashliyyah)} - \text{Immediate Due Debts}$$
     $$\text{If } \text{Net Income} \ge \text{Nisab} \implies \text{Zakat} = \text{Net Income} \times 2.5\%$$

### D. Zakat Pertanian & Buah-buahan (Agriculture / Zuru' wa Thimar)

- **Nisab**: 5 Wasaq = 300 Sa' $\approx$ **653 kg gabah (unhusked grain/rice)** or **520 kg beras (clean milled rice)**.
- **Hawl**: **No Hawl required**. Due immediately upon harvest day.
- **Irrigation Rates**:
  1. **Rain-fed / Natural springs / Flooding (Bighayri Kulfa)**: **10.0%** ($\frac{1}{10}$ / Al-'Usyr).
  2. **Artificial irrigation / Pumps / Purchased water (Bi Kulfa)**: **5.0%** ($\frac{1}{20}$ / Nisf al-'Usyr).
  3. **Mixed / Shared irrigation**: **7.5%** ($\frac{3}{40}$).

**Authoritative Dalil:**
- **Al-Qur'an Al-An'am (6:141)**:
  > وَآتُوا حَقَّهُ يَوْمَ حَصَادِهِ
  > *"And pay its due on the day of its harvest."*
- **Sahih Bukhari (No. 1483)**:
  > فِيمَا سَقَتِ السَّمَاءُ وَالْعُيُونُ أَوْ كَانَ عَثَرِيًّا الْعُشْرُ، وَمَا سُقِيَ بِالنَّضْحِ نِصْفُ الْعُشْرِ
  > *"On land irrigated by rain water or natural springs or if the land is wet due to water channels, one-tenth (10%) is compulsory, and on land irrigated by drawn water/camels/wells, half-of-one-tenth (5%) is compulsory."*
- **Sahih Bukhari (No. 1405), Sahih Muslim (No. 979)**:
  > لَيْسَ فِيمَا دُونَ خَمْسَةِ أَوْسُقٍ صَدَقَةٌ
  > *"There is no sadaqah (zakat) on anything less than five wasaq."*

### E. Zakat Hewan Ternak (Livestock / Al-An'am)

Conditions: **Sa'imah** (pastured freely on public pastures for at least 6 months of the year), and **Ghayr 'Amilah** (not working animals used for plowing or transport).

#### 1. Kambing / Domba (Goats / Sheep)
| Count Range | Required Zakat Due |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0 – 39** | **0** (Below Nisab) |
| **40 – 120** | **1 Syah** (1-year sheep/goat) |
| **121 – 200** | **2 Syah** |
| **201 – 300** | **3 Syah** |
| **301 – 400** | **4 Syah** |
| **> 300** | **+1 Syah for every additional 100 sheep/goats** |

*Sahih Bukhari (No. 1454)*:
> وَفِي صَدَقَةِ الْغَنَمِ فِي سَائِمَتِهَا إِذَا كَانَتْ أَرْبَعِينَ إِلَى عِشْرِينَ وَمِائَةٍ شَاةٌ، فَإِذَا زَادَتْ عَلَى عِشْرِينَ وَمِائَةٍ إِلَى مِائَتَيْنِ شَاتَانِ، فَإِذَا زَادَتْ عَلَى مِائَتَيْنِ إِلَى ثَلَاثِ مِائَةٍ فَفِيهَا ثَلَاثُ شِيَاهٍ، فَإِذَا زَادَتْ عَلَى ثَلَاثِ مِائَةٍ فَفِي كُلِّ مِائَةٍ شَاةٌ

#### 2. Sapi / Kerbau (Cows / Cattle / Buffalo)

### F. Zakat Rikaz & Ma'din (Buried Treasure & Minerals)

1. **Rikaz (Buried Ancient Treasure)**:
   - **Rate**: **20%** ($\frac{1}{5}$ / Al-Khumus).
   - **Hawl**: **No Hawl**.
   - **Nisab**: No nisab according to Jumhur (or 85g gold).
   - *Sahih Bukhari (No. 1499), Sahih Muslim (No. 1710)*:
     > وَفِي الرِّكَازِ الْخُمُسُ
     > *"And in rikaz [buried ancient treasure] there is one-fifth (20%)."*

2. **Ma'din (Extracted Minerals - Gold, Silver, Precious Ores)**:
   - **Rate**: **2.5%** upon extraction.
   - **Nisab**: Equivalent to 85g pure gold.
   - **Hawl**: No Hawl.

### G. Zakat Investasi Modern & Kripto (Modern Investments, Stocks, Crypto)

1. **Saham Trading (Active Day/Swing Trading)**:
   - Treated as 'Urud at-Tijarah (goods of trade).
   - Base = Portfolio market value + available cash + accrued dividends. Rate = 2.5% (Hijri) / 2.577% (Solar).
2. **Saham Jangka Panjang / Dividen (Passive Long-term Holding)**:
   - **AAOIFI Standard 35 Method**:
     $$\text{Zakat Per Share} = \frac{\text{Company's Net Zakatable Current Assets}}{\text{Total Shares Issued}}$$
   - **Islamic Fiqh Academy / Qaradawi Alternative**:
     If company data is unavailable, 10% on net received dividends (analogous to agricultural yield).
3. **Cryptocurrency & Digital Assets (BTC, ETH, Stablecoins, Tokens)**:
   - Evaluated as monetary wealth / 'Urud at-Tijarah at fair market price.
   - Nisab = 85g gold price.
   - Rate = 2.5% (Hijri) / 2.577% (Solar).

### H. Zakat Perusahaan / Korporasi (Corporate Zakat - AAOIFI Shari'ah Standard No. 35)

AAOIFI Standard No. 35 defines two mathematically equivalent balance-sheet methods:

1. **Metode Aset Lancar Bersih (Net Working Capital Approach)**:
   $$\text{Zakat Base} = (\text{Current Assets} - \text{Non-zakatable items}) - (\text{Current Liabilities used to finance current assets})$$
   - Add: Cash, marketable securities, trade receivables, trade inventory at net realizable value.
   - Deduct: Bad debts, short-term liabilities, non-Muslim equity share, government ownership share.

2. **Metode Modal Kerja Bersih (Net Financing Approach)**:
   $$\text{Zakat Base} = (\text{Paid-up Capital} + \text{Reserves} + \text{Retained Earnings} + \text{Long-term Debt}) - (\text{Net Fixed Assets} + \text{Long-term Investments} + \text{Intangibles})$$

$$\text{Zakat Due} = \text{Zakat Base} \times \text{Muslim Shareholding \%} \times (2.5\% \text{ Lunar or } 2.577\% \text{ Solar})$$

### I. Zakat Properti Sewaan & Mustaghallat (Rental Assets / Real Estate Yield)

- **Principal Asset**: Land and physical buildings are **exempt** from Zakat (capital assets not meant for trade).
- **Rental Yield**:
  $$\text{Net Rental Income} = \text{Gross Rental Income} - \text{Operational Expenses} - \text{Maintenance} - \text{Taxes}$$
  - **Option 1 (Qaradawi / Majma' al-Fiqh)**: 10% on net rental yield upon receipt (or 5% on gross rental).
  - **Option 2 (Jumhur / Classical)**: 2.5% after holding surplus income for 1 Hawl together with other monetary wealth.

## 4. Arithmetic Precision & Rounding Rules

1. **Floating Point Prohibition**: In monetary and Shari'ah calculations, standard 64-bit IEEE-754 binary floating-point math can lead to precision errors (e.g., $0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004$). All calculations must utilize high-precision arbitrary decimal representation.
2. **Rounding Mode**: Financial amounts default to `HALF_UP` or exact standard rounding to the minimum currency subunit (e.g., integer for IDR, 2 decimals for USD/EUR, 8 decimals for BTC).
3. **Livestock Units**: Livestock zakat outputs are whole positive integers. Fractional livestock is strictly invalid.
