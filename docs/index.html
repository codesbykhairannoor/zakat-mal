COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on Shari'ah calculations, technical implementation, and compliance standards.

Why is standard JavaScript floating-point arithmetic forbidden for Zakat?
JavaScript numbers use IEEE-754 64-bit binary floating-point representation. Calculations such as 0.1 + 0.2 === 0.30000000000000004 introduce cumulative drift in large balances. In Islamic jurisprudence, Zakat is a mandatory religious right of the poor (Haqq al-Fuqara') requiring exactness. zakat-mal implements a proprietary fixed-point decimal engine ensuring exact cent, penny, and gram precision.
How does AAOIFI Standard No. 35 adjust rates for solar (Gregorian) years?
The standard lunar year (Hijri) comprises ~354.36 days, for which the canonical rate is 2.500% (1/40). A Gregorian fiscal year comprises ~365.25 days. Section 4/1/4 of AAOIFI Shari'ah Standard No. 35 stipulates that for solar fiscal years, the rate is mathematically adjusted by multiplying 2.5% * (365.25 / 354.36) = 2.577% to ensure equality of religious obligation.
Should cash and bank savings be evaluated against the Gold or Silver Nisab?
Contemporary Islamic Fiqh bodies (AAOIFI, International Islamic Fiqh Academy, and Fatwa councils in Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Southeast Asia) predominantly benchmark cash wealth against the Gold Nisab (85 grams) because gold has historically maintained stable purchasing power representing the threshold of true surplus wealth (Al-Ghina).
Can zakat-mal run in Serverless, Edge, and Cloudflare Workers?
Yes! Because zakat-mal has zero external dependencies (dependencies: {}) and ships dual ESM (.mjs) and CJS (.js) builds, it boots with zero cold-start delay in Vercel Edge, Cloudflare Workers, AWS Lambda, Deno, Bun, and React Native mobile applications.
